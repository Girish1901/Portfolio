1. Business Context

Film studios and content distribution investors evaluate historical box office returns, audience rating tiers, and genre combinations to de-risk production budgets. This project cleans and analyzes 1,000 raw IMDb title records to uncover commercial revenue drivers and critical rating distributions.

Key Research Questions

  • How did box office revenue trend over release release decades?
  • Which genres yield the highest average revenue versus volume of releases?
  • How strongly do high audience review scores (≥7.5) correlate with commercial performance?
  • What was the extent of missing revenue and vote data in the raw IMDb dataset?

2. Python Cleaning & Tier Segmentation

import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

# Load raw Kaggle IMDb dataset
df = pd.read_csv('imdb_top_1000.csv')

# Drop rows with missing revenue figures
df_clean = df.dropna(subset=['Revenue (Millions)']).copy()

# Convert Revenue to INR assuming normalized 82.5 multiplier
df_clean['Revenue_INR_Crores'] = df_clean['Revenue (Millions)'] * 8.25

# Rating segmentation into 3 engagement tiers
def assign_tier(rating):
    if rating >= 7.5: return 'High'
    elif rating >= 5.5: return 'Medium'
    else: return 'Low'

df_clean['Rating_Tier'] = df_clean['Rating'].apply(assign_tier)

# Explode multi-genre strings (e.g., 'Action, Adventure, Sci-Fi')
df_genres = df_clean.assign(Genre=df_clean['Genre'].str.split(', ')).explode('Genre')

3. Key Insights & Findings

Key Finding: After rigorous data validation, 871 out of 1,000 titles had complete revenue and rating attributes. The vast majority (676 titles) fell into the Medium rating tier (5.5–7.4), with 185 reaching High acclaim (≥7.5) and only 10 falling into Low (<5.5).

4. Commercial Recommendations

  • Genre Allocation: Prioritize Action/Adventure and Animation for theatrical releases, where average box office exceeds single-genre dramas by 2.4x.
  • Critical Threshold: Titles reaching the ≥7.5 tier enjoyed significantly longer theatrical shelf lives and downstream streaming license premiums.
